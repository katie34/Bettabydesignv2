# bettabydesign

An interactive tool to help you learn the fundamentals of owning a pet fish. Understand what a betta needs to not just survive, but to thrive in the environment you create for it! Take a short pre-assessment before you start Lesson 1. It does not show pass or fail, so it records what you already know. After the lessons you will take a post-assessment and a short feedback survey.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

- Your progress is saved in this browser on this device. Please use the same browser and device for the whole course.
- Do not clear your browser's cache, cookies or site data, and do not use private or incognito mode, or your progress will be lost.
- Please do not look up answers or ask anyone for help during the assessments. They measure what you know, not how well you can search.

WHEN YOU FINISH

Download your results from the pre-assessment, the post-assessment and the Final Challenge, and email all three files to 'kkhan60@gatech.edu'. These files are how I match your results to your survey answers.

Link to site: https://github.gatech.edu/pages/kkhan60/bettabydesign/
